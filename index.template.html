AI Transparency

AI transparency and responsible use

Version: 24 August 2026

1. Scope

This notice describes the transparency principles Duevio should apply where an AI-assisted function is enabled. It does not state that every Duevio module uses AI and it does not classify any unverified function as high-risk.

2. Transparency at the point of use

Where an AI system is intended to interact directly with a natural person and the interaction is not obvious, the interface should clearly inform that person that they are interacting with an AI system, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act.

3. AI-generated or manipulated content

Where Duevio provides functions that generate or manipulate synthetic content, the provider and deployer responsibilities for machine-readable marking and user-facing disclosure should be assessed and implemented where Article 50 requires them.

4. Human oversight

AI-assisted outputs should be presented as support for qualified users. Compliance conclusions, customer risk decisions and regulatory actions require the organisation using Duevio to apply its own governance, policies and professional judgement.

5. AI literacy

Where Duevio personnel or customer personnel operate AI systems, the relevant provider or deployer should implement AI-literacy measures appropriate to the users' knowledge, experience and the context of use, as required by Article 4 of the EU AI Act.

6. Data protection

AI functionality that processes personal data remains subject to the GDPR. Controllers should assess lawful basis, transparency, data minimisation, security, retention, profiling and automated decision-making requirements before deployment.

7. Feature-specific assessment

Before any AI-assisted module is placed in production, its actual purpose, inputs, outputs, users and decision impact should be documented so the applicable role, risk classification and obligations under the AI Act can be determined from the real implementation.
